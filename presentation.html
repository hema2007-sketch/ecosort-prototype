01. The Problem 🚨

Current challenges in campus waste management

🗑️ Zero Sorting at Source

All types of waste are thrown into a single bin, making subsequent separation nearly impossible and highly inefficient.

💰 Wasted Economic Resources

Valuable recyclables (plastic, metal, paper) end up in landfills, losing significant potential revenue and harming the environment.

📊 No Data or Monitoring

Zero visibility on waste generation patterns, fill levels, or bin status, leading to overflowing bins and inefficient collection routes.

🤷‍♂️ No Student Engagement

Lack of an incentive system or awareness mechanism to encourage students to participate in sustainable practices.

03. How It Works ⚙️

Seamless automation from drop to data

Hardware Architecture

📷 Camera Module

🧠 Raspberry Pi (Brain)

⚙️ Servo & Rotating Disk

🗑️ 4 Compartments (Plastic, Metal, Paper, General)

  • Detect: Proximity sensor detects user.
  • Capture: Camera takes a photo of the item on the platform.
  • Classify: AI model identifies the material type.
  • Rotate: Disk rotates to align the correct compartment.
  • Drop: Trapdoor opens to drop the item.
  • Upload Data: Stats sent to cloud (Firebase).
  • Alert: Ultrasonic sensors alert staff at 80% capacity.

⏱️ Total Response Time < 2 Seconds

04. Smart Technology Track 🤖

Powered by AI and IoT

🧠 AI & IoT Specs

  • Models: YOLOv8 / MobileNetV2
  • Dataset: Trained on TrashNet & custom local data
  • Edge Computing: TensorFlow Lite for fast on-device inference
  • Accuracy: 95%+ classification accuracy
  • Microcontroller: ESP32 for sensor management
  • Connectivity: MQTT protocol & Firebase Realtime DB
  • Sensors: Ultrasonic fill-level sensors per compartment

📈 Admin Dashboard

  • Real-time bin status & location map
  • Collection route optimization
  • Waste generation analytics & charts
  • Automated alerts for full bins or maintenance
  • Exportable sustainability reports

Efficient, modular, and low-cost design

The Rotating Mechanism

Paper (180°)
Other (270°)
