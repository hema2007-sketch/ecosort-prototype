How It Works

Smart Sorting Process & AI Technology

  • Step 1: Student places waste in opening
  • Step 2: IR sensor detects the item
  • Step 3: Camera + AI classifies (95%+ accuracy)
  • Step 4: Servo rotates disk to correct angle
  • Step 5: Waste drops by gravity
  • Step 6: Data sent to cloud + points earned
  • Step 7: Auto-alert at 80% capacity
Note: Response time < 2 seconds

Technology Stack

  • AI Model: YOLOv8 / MobileNetV2
  • Dataset: TrashNet (2,527 images) + local data
  • On-device: TensorFlow Lite — works offline
  • Dual validation: Camera + Metal sensor = 95%+ accuracy
  • IoT: ESP32 ➔ MQTT ➔ Firebase Cloud
  • Active Learning Feedback Loop: Touchscreen for user feedback (Right/Wrong) sends edge cases to cloud, continuously retraining AI
  • Dashboard: Real-time map, charts, auto-alerts, reports
  • Smart route optimization: 30-45% fewer collection trips

Strategic Partnerships

The "Million Dollar" B2B Supply Chain Vision

EcoSort is not just a bin; it's a massive automated sorting infrastructure.

  • The Partnership: We partner with major recycling giants like BariQ, GoClean, and Bekia.
  • Value to Recyclers: Zero sorting costs, high-purity waste (plastic, aluminum), logistical efficiency from centralized university hubs.
  • Value to University: The university transforms from paying for waste disposal to getting paid for raw materials.
  • Scale: Scaling across all Cairo and Giza universities to supply industrial recyclers.
