# Business & Pitching Skill

This skill provides frameworks for structuring business ideas, creating pitch decks, and analyzing markets.

## 1. Business Model Canvas (BMC) Framework
When generating a BMC, always cover these 9 blocks in detail:
1. **Customer Segments:** Who are we creating value for? (B2B, B2C, B2B2C)
2. **Value Propositions:** What exact problem are we solving? What is the quantifiable benefit?
3. **Channels:** How do we reach our Customer Segments?
4. **Customer Relationships:** How do we get, keep, and grow customers?
5. **Revenue Streams:** How does the business make money? (One-time, subscription, asset sale, etc.)
6. **Key Resources:** What unique strategic assets must the business have? (Intellectual, physical, human, financial)
7. **Key Activities:** What uniquely strategic things does the business do?
8. **Key Partnerships:** Who are the key partners and suppliers?
9. **Cost Structure:** What are the major drivers of cost?

## 2. Pitch Deck Structure (10 Slides)
1. **Title:** Hook and Vision.
2. **Problem:** Pain points quantifiable with data.
3. **Solution:** The product and value prop.
4. **Why Now:** Timing and market trends.
5. **Market Size:** TAM, SAM, SOM.
6. **Competition:** Competitive matrix (why we are better).
7. **Product:** Demo or core features.
8. **Business Model:** Revenue and unit economics.
9. **Team:** Why this team is the right one.
10. **Financials/Ask:** Roadmap and what is needed.

## 3. Best Practices for Agents
- **Always quantify:** Use numbers instead of adjectives (e.g., "reduces cost by 30%" instead of "saves money").
- **Highlight unfair advantages:** E.g., proprietary datasets, exclusive partnerships, patents.
- **Focus on scalability:** Ensure the business model shows a clear path from pilot to mass market.
