The Core Problem

Waste mixing is destroying economic value before it even reaches the recycling plant.

  • Contamination: 70% of recyclables are ruined by organic waste mixing.
  • Lost Revenue: Millions of dollars burned in manual sorting facilities.
  • Inefficient Logistics: Bins are collected whether empty or overflowing, wasting fuel and time.

$120B+

Annual global economic value lost due to improper sorting at source.

Phase 1: The Smart Bin

Solving sorting at the exact moment of disposal.

AI Classification

Computer vision instantly identifies waste type (Plastic, Metal, Organic, Paper) with 98% accuracy.

Rotating Disk

Patented mechanical routing automatically directs the item into the correct internal compartment.

Interactive Screen

Active learning feedback loop. If the AI is unsure, the user taps the screen to train the model.

Status over Discounts

Shifting the motivation from extrinsic (cheap rewards) to intrinsic (identity, community, and official recognition).

The Scaling Masterplan

Turning universities into mini supply-chain hubs.

We are partnering with major recycling giants like BariQ and GoClean. Instead of them hunting for scattered recyclables, we provide a centralized, pre-sorted pipeline.

  • Zero Sorting Costs: Material is 99% pure upon collection.
  • Optimized Logistics: Bins trigger pickup requests only when full.
  • Revenue Sharing: The university earns a cut of the bulk material sales.

The Flow

Students Dispose via EcoSort
University Accumulates Pre-Sorted Tons
Direct Sale to BariQ/GoClean

Hyper-Local Circular Economy

NEW FEATURE

Closing the loop right on campus.

1. Collect

PET plastic bottles collected and shredded on-site via our advanced bins.

2. Extrude

Shredded plastic is melted and extruded into high-quality 3D printing filament.

3. Create

Filament sold/provided to engineering & architecture students for their prototypes.

Waste never leaves the campus. It becomes the building block for innovation.

Business Model

Diverse revenue streams and fast ROI.

  • B2B Bulk Sales: Selling high-purity sorted plastics and metals to recyclers.
  • 3D Filament Sales: High-margin recycled filament sold to university labs.
  • Dashboard SaaS: Subscription fee for universities to access ESG data, waste analytics, and carbon offset reports.

Unit Economics

Hardware cost recouped. Break-even in < 3 months per bin installation.
